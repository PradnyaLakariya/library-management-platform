# Stakeholder Matrix and User Personas

## 1. Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder Group | Key Roles | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Business Stakeholders | College management, library administrators, students, faculty | Define requirements, validate usability, and establish business goals |
| Engineering Stakeholders | Developers, UX designers, QA engineers | Design, develop, test, and maintain the application |
| Operations Stakeholders | DevOps engineers, database administrators, security teams | Manage infrastructure, deployment, data integrity, and security |

## 2. User Personas

### Persona 1: Aarav — Student

**Objectives**
- Find academic books quickly.
- Check book availability.
- Borrow and return books on time.
- Avoid unexpected overdue fines.

**Pain Points**
- Long queues at the library.
- Difficulty finding available books.
- Limited visibility into due dates and fines.

**Target Features**
- Book search and availability.
- Borrowing history.
- Due-date and fine information.

### Persona 2: Mrs. Sunita — Librarian

**Objectives**
- Maintain accurate catalog records.
- Issue and receive books efficiently.
- Track overdue books.
- Maintain reliable inventory information.

**Pain Points**
- Repetitive manual data entry.
- Inventory discrepancies.
- Time-consuming circulation records.

**Target Features**
- Book catalog management.
- Issue and return tracking.
- Overdue book and fine management.

### Persona 3: Dr. Verma — Library Administrator

**Objectives**
- Monitor library utilization.
- Review circulation and inventory reports.
- Identify missing or overdue books.
- Support informed operational decisions.

**Pain Points**
- Delayed reports.
- Limited real-time visibility.
- Manual compilation of operational data.

**Target Features**
- Administrative dashboard.
- Inventory and circulation summaries.
- Report generation.

## 3. Conclusion

The platform should address the different needs of students,
librarians, and administrators. These personas will guide future
design decisions and should be validated with actual users.
